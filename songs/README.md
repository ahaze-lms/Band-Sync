# Bundled songs

Public-domain melodies, transcribed by hand. The compositions themselves
are unambiguously public domain; the arrangements here are dedicated to
the public domain (CC0). Safe to bundle, distribute, modify, and use
commercially.

Generated by [`tools/build-songs.mjs`](../tools/build-songs.mjs) — edit
the note arrays there and re-run `node tools/build-songs.mjs` to
regenerate.

| File | Composition | Year | BPM | Tracks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `twinkle-twinkle.mid` | "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman" (French folk melody) | 1761 | 100 | Piano |
| `twinkle-twinkle-duet.mid` | (same composition, multi-track demo) | 1761 | 100 | Melody + Bass |
| `mary-had-a-little-lamb.mid` | Lowell Mason | 1830 | 96 | Piano |
| `ode-to-joy.mid` | Beethoven, 9th Symphony theme | 1824 | 112 | Piano |
| `frere-jacques.mid` | Traditional French round | 18th c. | 108 | Piano |
| `saints-go-marching-in.mid` | Traditional American spiritual | early 1900s | 120 | Piano |

All files are format-1 SMF, 480 PPQ. Single-track songs have one piano
track named "Piano"; the duet has separate "Melody" and "Bass" tracks
on channels 1 and 2 so the game's track picker can assign them to
different players.
